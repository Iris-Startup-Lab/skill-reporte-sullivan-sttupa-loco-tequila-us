1. Technical Audit and System Verdict

CURRENT DATA STATUS: INSUFFICIENT

The technical team completed an audit of the files in the Sullivan project workspace. The current data does not permit the generation of the Comparative vs. Plan Dashboard.

CRITICAL DEFECT 1

Missing Weekly Budget

Financial P&L statements contain only high-level monthly totals. There is no weekly budget broken down by SKU and sales channel.

Impact: Math cannot calculate % plan achievement or dollar variances.
CRITICAL DEFECT 2

No Continuous History

The workspace contains only isolated transactions for April 2026 and one week in August 2026. All 2025 transactions are missing.

Impact: Engine cannot calculate WoW, YoY, YTD, or 52-week trends.
CRITICAL DEFECT 3

Disconnected Data Silos

Commerce7 DTC orders, Wholesale Open POs, and Tock tasting bookings exist in separate unlinked formats.

Impact: Consolidated winery volume and revenue remain fragmented.

2. Five Critical Data Gaps Sullivan Must Resolve

Actionable Requirements
GAP 01

Deliver Weekly Plan / Budget File

Sullivan must provide a budget file structured by ISO week (e.g. 2026-W16), canonical wine product name, sales channel, target bottles, and target net revenue in USD.

  • Define targets for each commercial channel.
  • Define targets for each primary wine SKU.
  • Do not supply monthly totals without weekly allocation.
GAP 02

Deliver Continuous Historical Sales Data

The analytics engine requires historical continuity to calculate comparative trends.

  • Provide all 52 weeks of calendar year 2025.
  • Provide all continuous weeks of 2026 up to current week.
  • Do not omit quiet weeks. Record zero volume weeks explicitly.
GAP 03

Unify Commercial Sales Channels

Consolidate DTC orders (Commerce7), wholesale invoices (distributors), and tasting experiences (Tock) into one unified row-level table.

  • Every row must represent one sold product line.
  • Include date, product SKU, channel, volume, and net revenue.
GAP 04

Standardize Channel Names (Commerce7 Logic)

Apply Sullivan's official classification rules from April_C7_Data_Guide.xlsx.

  • Map POS orders to Tasting Room.
  • Map Club orders to Estate Club or Founder's Club.
  • Classify Inbound tags into Telesales, Event, or Corporate.
GAP 05

Isolate Physical Bottles from Service Fees and Discounts

Transaction exports contain wine tastings, event fees, and shipping surcharges. Add a mandatory Categoria_Negocio column to distinguish physical bottles.

  • Assign Producto (Botellas) to physical wine bottles.
  • Assign Experiencias / Tastings to tasting reservations.
  • Assign Servicios / Cuotas to shipping and processing fees.
  • This isolation prevents distortion of bottle volume and average bottle price.

6. Commerce7 Channel Classification Rulebook

Taxonomy Rulebook

Sullivan defines strict channel taxonomy in April_C7_Data_Guide.xlsx. Raw exports must be mapped to these exact channel names in the Canal column:

Commerce7 Raw Source / Tag Standardized Report Channel (Canal) Classification Logic
POS terminal at tasting room Tasting Room All sales rung through tasting room point-of-sale hardware.
Club with title matching "Estate" Estate Club Automatic bi-annual club shipments for Estate tier members.
Club with title matching "Founder" Founder's Club Allocated club shipments for Founder's Reserve members.
Inbound with tag Event Event Special on-site dinners and private events.
Inbound with tag Corporate Corporate Corporate gift orders and executive gifting accounts.
Inbound with tag Friends & Family Friends & Family Staff, family, and industry partner courtesy purchases.
Inbound without special tags Telesales Direct sales via phone, VIP concierge, or email campaign.
Web with payment source matching Tock Tock Prepaid hospitality experiences and tasting reservation deposits.
Web standard cart checkout Web / Ecommerce Public online bottle purchases through sullivanwine.com.
Wholesale invoices / Open POs / Depletions Wholesale Distributor distribution (Southern Glazer's, RNDC, out-of-state).

Rule on Taxonomy: The channel names above are official business terms. Do not translate these names into Spanish or any other language; they match Commerce7 registers exactly.
